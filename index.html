About the conference

Where research meets real-world systems

TechFusion brings together researchers, practitioners and students working across intelligent systems, sustainable engineering and applied computing for two days of talks, peer-reviewed papers and cross-disciplinary conversation.

Now in its fourth edition, TechFusion is an international conference that examines how intelligent systems — from machine learning to autonomous infrastructure — can be built responsibly and deployed at scale, without losing sight of environmental and social cost.

The conference pairs a peer-reviewed technical track with keynotes and panels from academia and industry, giving early-career researchers a direct line to people building these systems today.

01

Provide a rigorous, peer-reviewed venue for original research in intelligent and sustainable systems.

02

Connect early-career researchers with established academics and industry practitioners.

03

Surface applied, deployable work — not only theoretical contributions.

04

Publish accepted work through an indexed conference proceedings partner.

Call for papers

Submit your research to TechFusion 2027

We welcome original, unpublished work — full papers, short papers and work-in-progress reports — across the following tracks.

Artificial Intelligence & ML

Deep learning, NLP, computer vision, generative models and applied AI systems.

IoT & Edge Computing

Embedded systems, sensor networks, edge inference and real-time architectures.

Sustainable Computing

Green infrastructure, energy-aware systems, climate and clean-energy applications.

Cybersecurity & Privacy

Applied cryptography, threat detection, secure systems design and data privacy.

Robotics & Automation

Autonomous systems, industrial automation, human–robot interaction.

Cloud, Data & Distributed Systems

Distributed architectures, data engineering, large-scale systems and analytics.

Submission process

01

Prepare your manuscript

Format your paper using the standard two-column IEEE conference template, 6–8 pages including references.

02

Submit through the portal

Upload your PDF via the conference submission system along with author details and track selection.

03

Peer review

Each submission receives at least two double-blind reviews from the technical program committee.

04

Notification of acceptance

Authors are notified by email on 8 December 2026. The notification includes the decision, reviewer comments, presentation format (oral or poster) and any revisions requested.

05

Camera-ready & registration

Accepted authors submit a revised camera-ready copy and complete registration to be included in proceedings.

Publication details

  • Accepted papers published in indexed conference proceedings
  • Selected best papers fast-tracked to a partner journal special issue
  • Best Paper and Best Student Paper awards per track
  • All authors receive a digital certificate of presentation
Notification details: decisions are sent by email to the corresponding author on 8 December 2026, along with reviewer feedback and the assigned presentation format. Check your spam folder and add techfusion2027@vmrfchennai.edu.in to your contacts to avoid missing it.
